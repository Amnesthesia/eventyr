Over the past decade, Body Type have established themselves as one of Australia’s most acclaimed and compelling bands. Since forming in Sydney in 2016, they have released two celebrated albums, earned an Australian Music Prize nomination, toured extensively throughout Australia, Europe and the UK, and shared stages with Foo Fighters, Fontaines D.C., Pixies, Big Thief, Warpaint, Sleater-Kinney and Cate Le Bon. Now, the four-piece return with Tally, their third album and most ambitious work to date. Marking the band’s tenth anniversary, Tally captures a group taking stock, embracing change and pushing their sound into bold new territory while retaining the wit, charm and sharp observations that have become their trademark. Recorded in Los Angeles with acclaimed producer Stella Mozgawa (Warpaint, Courtney Barnett), Tally draws inspiration from a wide range of sources: the work of artist Louise Bourgeois, misty drives through the Hollywood Hills, 10 years of history as a band. The album transforms the everyday into something surreal, witty and profound while exploring love, friendship, insecurity and the strange beauty hidden within ordinary life.

Rob McWilliams Band Professor Rob McWilliams has led an interesting life. A musician and composer, playing trumpet, flugelhorn and piano, he has been part of the jazz scene for many years, originally playing professionally in Melbourne with artists such as Wilbur Wilde and Paul Grabowsky. His studies and high level of musicianship led him to the USA where he took up the post of Professor of Music at the University of Wisconsin, Oshkosh and ended up as Head of Music there for several years. As a teacher he has presented at major music conferences in Japan, Hong Kong, the UK, Singapore, Europe, China and Australia and is a published author. As a musician he has performed with many jazz greats including Nat Adderley, Vincent Herring, Dee Dee Bridgewater, Eddie Daniels, Ed Shaughnessy and Dale Barlow – a resume that is the envy of many an Australian jazz musician! A talented musician, composer and arranger, Rob has had many opportunities over the years to create and perform his music to audiences all over the world. Now living back in Australia, he works for Yamaha Music conducting music workshops around Australia as well as teaching at the Jazz Music Institute in Brisbane, conducting, composing, arranging music and performing regularly around Australia either as a side man or with his own jazz ensemble. Though he is a Professor with an incredible and impressive Curriculum Vitae, he is a warm and down-to-earth performer with a genuine connection to the audience, bringing the music alive. This masterful musician in now bringing his music to the Sunshine Coast, performing with his band at The Jazz Sessions at The Presynct on Thurs 17th Sep. Joined on this gig by a stellar line-up of local Brisbane & Sunshine Coast musicians, they have come together to perform unique interpretations of jazz standards and some of Rob’s original compositions. Be prepared for an evening of sophistication, smooth jazz and world-class musicianship.
