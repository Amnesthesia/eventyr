### 🎓 [Italian Prisoners of War on Queensland Farms – Talk by Joanne Tapiolas](https://www.slq.qld.gov.au/)
**Sun 17 May** · 📍 Anzac Square Memorial Galleries, 285 Ann Street, Brisbane City · 💰 Free
`history` `wwii` `lecture` `free` `queensland` `community`

Historian Joanne Tapiolas presents research on the little-known program that placed unguarded Italian POWs on Queensland farms during WWII to address rural labour shortages. The talk draws on personal accounts from farming families and the prisoners themselves, revealing an unexpectedly human story from the home front. Attendees can stay to explore the Anzac Square Memorial Galleries after the session.

> 💡 A genuinely niche slice of Queensland wartime history told through personal stories — the kind of specific, well-researched talk that rarely gets mainstream attention.
