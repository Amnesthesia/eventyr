### 🎓 [Italian POWs in Queensland: Stories from the Home Front](https://www.slq.qld.gov.au/)
📆 Sun 17 May
📍 Anzac Square Memorial Galleries, 285 Ann Street, Brisbane City
💰 Free
`history` `wwii` `queensland` `lecture` `free` `community`

Independent researcher Joanne Tapiolas presents her research on Italian prisoners of war interned on Queensland farms during WWII — an unconventional chapter of history in which unguarded POWs lived and worked in nine rural Queensland districts. The talk draws on personal stories: POWs teaching children to twirl spaghetti, singing lullabies, crafting a girl's first new shoes. A deeply humanising, little-known slice of Australian and Queensland history.
