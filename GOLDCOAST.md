After multiple sold-out seasons across Australia and a 5-star review at Fringe World Perth , Unsettled: The Search Between Wandering & Belonging returns to the Gold Coast, for a special one-night performance. One woman. One suitcase. Too many existential crisis. What happens when home is no longer a place you can find on a map? Unsettled: The Search Between Wandering & Belonging is a fearless, funny, and deeply moving one-woman show about chasing dreams, getting lost along the way, and building a new life from scratch. With humour and intimate honesty, Agustina takes you on a wild ride through the heartbreak, comedy, and chaos of living abroad. Vulnerable, hilarious, and fiercely alive, Unsettled invites you to feel seen, laugh, cry, and maybe even find a piece of yourself along the way. What began as a street performance in Port Douglas has evolved into a multi-city, sold-out show , touring Magnetic Island, Airlie Beach, Noosa, Byron Bay, Gold Coast, Margaret River, and successful sell out seasons at Adelaide Fringe, Fringe World Perth, Sydney Fringe & Melbourne Fringe. Following a successful Byron Bay season, with nine performances and multiple sold-out nights, Unsettled heads just up the coast to Gold Coast. After previously appearing at Miami Marketta as part of HOLA Marketta the Winter Latin Festival , the show returns for a special standalone performance, bringing its playful storytelling to new audiences. This time, experience it in a cozy, intimate venue, perfect for getting up close and personal with the story. ⚠️Limited capacity. One night only.
