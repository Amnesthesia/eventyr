#### [The Butterfly Who Flew Into The Rave](https://bleachfestival.com.au/events/bleach-26-the-butterfly-who-flew-into-the-rave)
📆 Fri 2 Oct – Sun 4 Oct
📍 Basement
💰 See link
`performance` `dance` `theatre` `music`

This electrifying performance explores rave culture in a hypnotic hour-long spectacle of pure endurance. This award-winning durational work is choreographed to the techno album 'Nocturbulous Behaviour' by Suburban Knights and straddles the border between performance art, dance, and theatre. Three dancers are locked in a pattern of relentless movement, in seamless synch, detailed down to every beat. It’s the come up and the come down all in one - the beauty of feeling alive, but the consequences that come with it. The dancers embody the obsession of rave culture, perfectly capturing the communal joy and the physical toll of dancing until you drop. Indulge in the pain, the sweat, the cathartic mess; a display of pure endurance to achieve a goal. A spectacle of the human body as a victim to music, as a victim to passion, as a victim to our endless desire to achieve more. To win and win again.

#### [Ocker](https://bleachfestival.com.au/events/bleach-26-ocker)
📆 Sat 3 Oct – Mon 5 Oct
📍 HOTA Lake Precinct
💰 See link
`circus` `performance` `theatre`

Join us for the world premiere of Ocker, a show in motion from leading First Nations company Na Djinang Circus. Ocker is a roving circus show tearing through the precinct on a portable railway track built in real time. This modern-day hunting party weaves through crowds, laying track wherever the chase leads. Charging forward with single-minded determination, they drag the audience along for the ride. The park becomes a playground for strange encounters, risky acrobatics, and absurd acts of endurance as circus erupts in the most unexpected places. Improvised scenes constantly derail the hunt as the troupe negotiates crowds, obstacles, and their own terrible ideas. Swept up in the chaos of the chase, Ocker waits for nobody. Keeping up is entirely your responsibility.
